About the Center

The Mental Health & Medication Assisted Center is a Los Angeles practice offering therapy, behavioral treatment and psychiatric medication services.

We see adults facing anxiety, depression, ADHD, trauma and mood disorders, and we tailor recommendations to each person rather than assuming one approach fits everyone.

When medication is part of the plan, decisions are made by a prescribing provider after assessment, with regular follow-up to review how things are going.

We are open Monday through Friday from 9 am to 6 pm, and our office is on Wilshire Boulevard in central Los Angeles.

About Mental Health &amp; Medication Assisted, Center

Counseling and Medication Services on Wilshire Blvd

The Mental Health & Medication Assisted Center was set up to give people a coordinated place to seek mental health care in Los Angeles. We offer therapy and psychiatric medication services together, which can make it easier to follow a plan and keep everyone informed.

Our approach is holistic: we look at symptoms, history, daily stress and strengths, then recommend a combination of counseling and, where clinically appropriate, medication. If you or someone near you is in immediate danger or crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.
